Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who uses this app

rclone archive is used only by its owner, Ishmael Akorley, to back up his own files to his own Google Drive. It has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

It uses one Google permission: see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files it creates. It cannot see any other files in the Drive.

How the data is used

Files are copied from the owner's computer to the owner's Google Drive, and nothing else. No data is sold, shared, sent to anyone else, or used for advertising or AI training.

Where the data is stored

The files stay in the owner's Google Drive. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer, in rclone's own settings file.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account > Security > Your connections to third-party apps and services.
